Privacy Policy

Last updated —

Introduction

Your privacy matters. This Privacy Policy explains how we, SillyLittleTech, collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you visit our lander, and affiliated project pages.         




Information We Collect


We collect only the information needed to respond to inquiries, and to understand how to improve our site.



  • Contact form details: When you submit the "Contact Us" form, we receive the name you share, your email address, and the contents of your message. The form routes through Pageclip's secure serves. You can view how they handle submissions in their Privacy Policy.

  • Request and Performance logs: Cloudflare Pages logs standard metadata (such as IP address information, use agent, and the requested URLs visited to keep the site running reliably and diagnose issues. These logs are retained briefly and are not used for any marketing purposes.

  • Zero Trust Access Events: Cloudflare Zero Trust (Access) and Okta records when administrative tools are used in the project, for security auditing purposes. These events are tied to Volenteer and Administrative services and are not shared externally.



How We Use your information



  • Responding to you directly when you reach out through the Contact form.
  • Monitoring site performance, debugging issues, and improving features through aggregated analyitical insights.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of our Volenteer and Administrative infrastructure.


Services and Third Parties


The lander page is hosted through Cloudflare Pages and admin tools are protected with Cloudflare Zero Trust Access (SSO through Okta). These services process data on our behalf to deliver site, apply policies, and monitor for abuse. You can review their practices here:


  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

  • Cloudflare Pages Data Usage

  • Okta Privacy Policy


Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)



If you reside in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we reply on the following legal bases under the General Data Protection Regulation:



  • Consent for analytics and form submissions; you can withdraw/request your data be deleted from our systems at any time by contacting us.

  • Legitimate interest in operation and security of our projects and services


Your Privacy Rights


Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data.



  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You can request a copy of the data collected about you, ask for deletion, or opt out of data sharing. We do not sell personal data, as defined by California law.

  • EEA & UK Residents (GDPR): You can request access to your personal data, correction of inaccuracies, deletion, or a copy for portability. You may also object to or request restriction of certain processing.


    • To exercise any of these rights, email us at gdpr@sillylittle.tech.



Data Renention



Contact messages are retained only for as long as needed to respond to you or maintain a conversation record. Aggregated analytics data is stored according to our default retention schdedule, which is currently 14 months, unless a short period trumps it.



Security



Some of our projects may use Firebase (by Google), which encrypts information in transit and at rest. We also personally review analytical data and access logs to monitor for unexpected abuse. No system can be perfectly secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.



International Data Transfers



We process our data on servers located in the United States and limited other countries. These transfers follow Cloudflare's global infrastructure policy and incorperate appropriate safeguards.



Changes to This Policy



We may update this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes, new legal requirements, or provider changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a Last Updated data, so please check back regularly. We will try, but cannot guarantee, an advanced notice.



Contact


Questions about this policy or your data rights? Reach out to us any time at privacy@sillylittle.tech
