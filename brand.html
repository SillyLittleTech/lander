How do we look?

Our Brand Principals

Simply Readable

Create clean, open, designs that are easily readable from far away.

Let it Flow

Tailor designs like liquid, let assets magically meet together with charm and personality.

Stay Distinct

Each asset should feel connected but be visually distinct and unique.

Create delight

Evoke wonder and discovery.

Details matter

Achieve the highest levels of quality and craft.

As with our products, every detail matters. Each moment is an opportunity to elevate quality.

Logo usage & policy

We are a not-for-profit project. Only affiliated projects should use our logo with our permission. If you wish to use the logo for marketing, please contact us at the project email listed in our repo.

  • Do not modify the logo mark or colors.
  • Always provide adequate clear space around the mark.
  • Prefer the raster PNG provided in assetlab/raster for web use.
